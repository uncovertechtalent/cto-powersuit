# Example calibrations (not defaults)

Three fitted filters for three different people. None of them is yours; they exist to show the spectrum of valid calibrations, because a single starter menu makes the catalog look like a fixed thing with one right answer. Derive your own with `/calibrate`.

## Engineering lead

Wants directness without losing technical precision. Keeps calibrated uncertainty.

- Catalog: praise openers, service closers, validators-before-disagreement, preamble. All deleted.
- KEPT deliberately: hedges attached to genuinely uncertain claims ("likely", "in most configs"), because their reports feed decisions.
- Register: full grammar, technical vocabulary untouched, short paragraphs.
- Level: full. Auto-clarity: code reviews and incident writeups get normal English.
- Hook: praise-opener, service-closer only. Everything else prose.

## Technical writer

Wants clean drafts, not compressed ones. The filter's job is removing noise, not style.

- Catalog: filler idioms, buzzwords ("leverage", "robust", "seamless"), preamble, moralizing on factual topics.
- KEPT deliberately: transitions ("however", "therefore"), warmth in teaching contexts, illustrative examples.
- Register: normal prose; sentence variety is a feature, not a vestige.
- Level: lite. Auto-clarity: everything user-facing they're drafting gets no filter at all (their published voice is their own).
- Hook: filler-idiom, buzzword list. Low block rate by design.

## Solo dev

Wants maximum signal per token in a fast loop.

- Catalog: everything in the starter menu, plus emotional mirroring and consensus framings.
- KEPT deliberately: nothing. Errors quoted verbatim per boundaries.
- Register: telegram. Fragments, dropped articles, arrows for causality in scratch work.
- Level: ultra default, `filter lite` spoken for anything they'll paste to another human. Auto-clarity: security warnings, destructive-action confirmations, multi-step migrations in full English.
- Hook: aggressive; false positives accepted, one-word overrides cheap.

The pattern across all three: what gets KEPT is as calibrated as what gets removed, and auto-clarity boundaries differ more between people than the ban lists do.
